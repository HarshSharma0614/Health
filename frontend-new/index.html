About AuraCare Predictor

System Overview & End-to-End Operational Capacity Recovery Pipeline

AI-Assisted Scheduling Decision Support

AuraCare Predictor provides operational decision support for hospital scheduling staff. The system bridges machine learning risk prediction with real-time waitlist matching to ensure empty slots are rapidly backfilled with compatible patients.

Human-in-the-Loop Principle: The system does NOT automatically cancel appointments or alter patient care priorities. All scheduling recommendations require human staff verification.
